# Germany — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, agencies, and small companies billing from Germany.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **19%**.
- Key reduced rate: **7%**; most professional services stay at 19%.
- Domestic VAT small-business threshold: from **2025** the **Kleinunternehmer** regime is generally described with a **€25,000** prior-year / start-year threshold framework and a higher current-year ceiling. Verify the current exact rule before relying on it.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without German VAT** under reverse charge if the customer is a taxable business in another Member State.
- Include VAT IDs when relevant.
- Typical wording: `Steuerschuldnerschaft des Leistungsempfängers / Reverse charge — Art. 196 Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-location VAT and may require **OSS** once the EU cross-border threshold is exceeded.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- supplier and customer names/addresses
- invoice date and unique number
- tax number or VAT ID of the supplier
- customer VAT ID for intra-EU B2B reverse-charge cases
- description and date/period of service
- net amount, VAT rate/amount or exemption wording
- total due
- if using **Kleinunternehmer** treatment, use the correct no-VAT wording instead of charging VAT

Recommended practical fields:
- payment terms
- email / phone / bank details
- PO/reference number

Treat the list above as a practical split between likely mandatory/core details and commonly useful extras. If a German exemption wording or extra identifier is uncertain, verify before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- Quotes / offers should say whether prices are **net** or **gross**.
- State validity period, assumptions, and any advance-payment milestone clearly.

## Sole trader / company note
- The billing difference that matters most is whether the seller is under **Kleinunternehmer** treatment or regular VAT. Do not assume based on entity type alone.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing may be **monthly, quarterly, or annual** depending on prior VAT liability and regime.
- Preserve evidence for intra-EU B2B status and any OSS sales.

## Escalate when
- the seller is near the **Kleinunternehmer** boundary or has just crossed it
- the invoice needs German-specific exemption wording you are unsure about
- e-invoicing acceptance/sending obligations affect the workflow
