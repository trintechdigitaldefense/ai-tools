# Malta — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, and small companies billing from Malta.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **18%**.
- Reduced rates exist in specific sectors; most professional services use 18%.
- Malta has domestic VAT-registration thresholds and separate intra-EU / digital-service considerations; verify the current threshold before relying on non-registration.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Maltese VAT** under reverse charge if the customer's country is the place of supply.
- Include VAT IDs where relevant.
- Typical wording: `Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-location VAT and may require **OSS**.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- invoice number and issue date
- seller legal name/address
- seller VAT ID if registered
- customer legal name/address
- customer VAT ID for reverse-charge B2B cases
- description, tax base, VAT treatment, total

Recommended practical fields:
- service period
- payment terms
- contact details and bank details

Mandatory versus recommended details can differ by registration status and local practice. Verify exact Maltese no-VAT wording before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- State whether values are net or VAT included.
- Add validity period, scope, exclusions, and deposit terms.

## Sole trader / company note
- The critical billing question is VAT-registration status, not just entity type.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing is commonly **quarterly** once registered.
- Keep records supporting intra-EU B2B treatment and any OSS sales.

## Escalate when
- the seller is near the threshold
- cross-border services are supplied while registration status is unclear
- exact Maltese wording is needed
