# Spain — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For autónomos, consultants, contractors, agencies, and small Spanish companies billing from Spain.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **21%**.
- Key reduced rates: **10%** and **4%**; most professional services use 21%.
- Domestic VAT registration threshold: **Spain does not generally operate a domestic small-business VAT threshold** like some other EU states. If you make taxable supplies, assume VAT registration may be needed from the start.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Spanish VAT** under reverse charge when the customer is the place of supply.
- VAT numbers matter and may require the seller to be correctly enrolled for intra-EU operations.
- Typical wording: `Inversión del sujeto pasivo / Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** usually follow consumer-country VAT. OSS can simplify reporting once the EU cross-border threshold is exceeded.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- supplier legal name/address and tax/VAT ID
- customer details and VAT ID for intra-EU B2B cases
- invoice number and issue date
- service description and supply date/period
- taxable base, VAT rate/amount or no-VAT wording, total due
- payment terms
- keep the invoice wording clear if reverse charge applies

Recommended practical fields:
- email / phone / bank details
- PO/reference number

Treat the list above as a practical split between likely mandatory/core details and commonly useful extras. If Spanish exemption wording, ROI/intra-EU setup, or extra identifier requirements are uncertain, verify before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- Make clear whether prices are **sin IVA** or **IVA incluido**.
- State validity, assumptions, and deposit / milestone terms.

## Sole trader / company note
- Many Spanish freelancers use the **autónomo** structure; billing risk often comes less from entity type and more from VAT registration, census status, and cross-border setup.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- Small businesses often work on a **quarterly VAT rhythm** plus annual summary/reconciliation tasks.
- Keep good records for intra-EU operations and customer VAT evidence.

## Escalate when
- the seller has not completed the right intra-EU setup but wants to issue reverse-charge invoices
- the invoice mixes Spanish domestic work and non-Spanish place-of-supply work
- digital services / OSS or platform rules are involved
