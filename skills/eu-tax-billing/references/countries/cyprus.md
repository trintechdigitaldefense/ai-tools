# Cyprus — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, and small companies billing from Cyprus.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **19%**.
- Common reduced rates: **9%** and **5%** in specific sectors; most professional services stay at 19%.
- Cyprus has a domestic VAT-registration threshold and separate cross-border triggers; verify the current threshold and whether cross-border services trigger earlier registration.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Cyprus VAT** under reverse charge if the customer's country is the place of supply.
- Include VAT IDs where relevant.
- Typical wording: `Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-location VAT and may require **OSS**.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- invoice number and issue date
- seller legal name/address
- seller VAT ID if registered
- customer legal name/address
- customer VAT ID for reverse-charge B2B cases
- description, tax base, VAT treatment, total

Recommended practical fields:
- service period
- payment terms
- email / phone / bank details

Do not assume phone, email, or company number are mandatory unless confirmed. If relying on non-registration or exemption wording, verify the current Cyprus rules first.

## Quote notes
- Clarify whether amounts are before VAT or VAT included.
- Add validity period, scope, exclusions, and deposit terms.

## Sole trader / company note
- Confirm actual VAT-registration status; entity type alone does not answer the billing question.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing is commonly **quarterly** once registered.
- Keep support for intra-EU B2B status and any OSS records.

## Escalate when
- cross-border services are supplied while VAT registration status is unclear
- the seller is near the registration threshold
- local wording or filing rhythm needs certainty
