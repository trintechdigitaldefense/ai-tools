# Denmark — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, agencies, and small companies billing from Denmark.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **25%**.
- Denmark generally does **not** use multiple broad reduced VAT rates for most invoices; many professional services simply use 25%.
- Denmark has a domestic VAT-registration threshold; verify the current threshold before relying on non-registration.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Danish VAT** under reverse charge if the customer's country is the place of supply.
- Include VAT IDs where relevant.
- Typical wording: `Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-location VAT and may require **OSS**.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- invoice number and issue date
- seller legal name/address
- seller VAT number if registered
- customer legal name/address
- customer VAT number for reverse-charge B2B cases
- description, taxable amount, VAT treatment, total

Recommended practical fields:
- service period
- payment terms
- email / phone / bank details

Use the country file cautiously: this is a practical split between core and useful fields, not a substitute for current Danish invoicing rules. Verify exact no-VAT wording if the seller is below threshold.

## Quote notes
- State clearly whether prices are before or after VAT.
- Include validity period, scope, and deposit or milestone terms if relevant.

## Sole trader / company note
- Entity type matters less than VAT-registration status for invoice wording.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing may be **monthly, quarterly, or half-yearly** depending on turnover.
- Keep support for cross-border B2B treatment and any OSS position.

## Escalate when
- the seller is near the registration threshold
- the business has mixed domestic and cross-border revenue
- exact Danish wording or filing frequency is needed
