# Netherlands — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For Dutch zzp'ers, consultants, agencies, contractors, and small companies billing from the Netherlands.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **21%**.
- Key reduced rate: **9%**; most professional services use 21%.
- Domestic VAT registration threshold: **there is no general VAT-registration threshold** for carrying on taxable business. The main small-business relief is the **KOR**, generally available up to **€20,000** turnover under conditions.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Dutch VAT** under reverse charge when the customer is the place of supply.
- Include VAT IDs where relevant.
- Typical wording: `btw verlegd / Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow the consumer's country VAT. Use **OSS** if the EU cross-border threshold is exceeded.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- supplier legal name/address and VAT number if registered
- customer details and VAT number for reverse-charge B2B cases
- invoice date and sequential number
- description and service date/period
- taxable amount, VAT rate/amount or reverse-charge note, total due
- payment terms
- if under **KOR**, do not show VAT as charged

Recommended practical fields:
- email / phone / bank details
- PO/reference number

Treat the list above as a practical split between likely mandatory/core details and commonly useful extras. If a Dutch KOR wording or extra identifier is uncertain, verify before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- State whether prices are **excl. btw** or **incl. btw**.
- Include validity and clear assumptions; Dutch clients often expect that to be explicit.

## Sole trader / company note
- For Dutch small businesses, the main billing distinction is usually **KOR vs normal VAT**, not sole trader vs BV.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- Many Dutch businesses file VAT **quarterly**; monthly or annual patterns also exist depending on circumstances.
- Keep support for reverse-charge invoices and OSS records.

## Escalate when
- the user may want KOR but also wants to reclaim input VAT
- domestic Dutch VAT and cross-border B2C digital sales are mixed
- platform / marketplace rules may shift the deemed-supplier treatment
