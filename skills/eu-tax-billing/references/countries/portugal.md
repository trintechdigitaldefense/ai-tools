# Portugal — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For Portuguese freelancers, contractors, consultants, agencies, and small companies billing from Portugal.

## VAT basics
- Mainland standard VAT: **23%**.
- Key reduced rates: **13%** and **6%** on the mainland; regional rates differ in Madeira/Azores.
- Domestic VAT exemption threshold: the **Article 53** small-business exemption has been changing. A common current working number is **€15,000** turnover, but verify the present threshold and transition rules before relying on it.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Portuguese VAT** under reverse charge when the customer's country is the place of supply.
- Include VAT numbers where relevant.
- Typical wording: `IVA - autoliquidação / Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-country VAT and can trigger **OSS** once relevant cross-border turnover is reached.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- seller legal name/address and tax/VAT number
- customer details and VAT number for B2B reverse-charge cases
- invoice number and issue date
- description and service date/period
- taxable base, VAT rate/amount or exemption wording, total due
- payment terms
- if using **Article 53** exemption, use the correct local no-VAT wording rather than charging VAT

Recommended practical fields:
- email / phone / bank details
- PO/reference number

Treat the list above as a practical split between likely mandatory/core details and commonly useful extras. If Portuguese exemption wording, SAF-T/software constraints, or extra identifier requirements are uncertain, verify before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- State whether the quote is **com IVA** or **sem IVA**.
- Include validity, scope, and deposit / milestone terms.

## Sole trader / company note
- For Portuguese independents, the billing-critical point is often whether **Article 53** exemption still applies, not just whether they are a sole trader or company.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- Expect **monthly or quarterly** VAT filing depending on turnover/regime.
- Keep cross-border evidence and OSS records if relevant.

## Escalate when
- the seller is near the current **Article 53** boundary
- mainland vs island VAT rates matter
- the business has mixed exempt, domestic, and foreign revenue
