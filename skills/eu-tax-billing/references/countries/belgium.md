# Belgium — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For Belgian freelancers, consultants, contractors, management companies, and small service businesses.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **21%**.
- Key reduced rates sometimes relevant on invoices: **12%** and **6%**; most professional services use 21%.
- Domestic VAT exemption threshold for small businesses: **€25,000** annual turnover is the key working number.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Belgian VAT** under reverse charge when the customer is the place of supply.
- Put both VAT numbers on the invoice when relevant.
- Typical wording: `Autoliquidation / Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-country VAT. OSS becomes relevant once cross-border B2C digital turnover exceeds the EU-wide threshold.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- seller legal name/address and VAT number if registered
- customer legal name/address and VAT number for B2B reverse-charge cases
- invoice number and date
- clear description, quantity/time, and service date/period
- taxable base, VAT rate/amount or exemption note, total due
- payment terms
- if the Belgian small-business exemption applies, use the correct no-VAT wording rather than a VAT rate

Recommended practical fields:
- email / phone / bank details
- PO/reference number

Treat the list above as a practical split between likely mandatory/core details and commonly useful extras. If a local exemption wording, registration number, or country-specific field is uncertain, verify before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- State whether the quote is **excl. VAT** or **incl. VAT**.
- Add validity, scope boundaries, and deposit / milestone terms.

## Sole trader / company note
- Many Belgian one-person businesses operate either as sole traders or via a BV/SRL, but the billing-critical question is still: VAT-exempt small-business regime or normal VAT regime?

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing is commonly **monthly or quarterly** depending on turnover/profile.
- Keep customer VAT-number evidence for intra-EU B2B invoices.

## Escalate when
- turnover is near the €25,000 threshold
- there is mixed domestic VAT, reverse-charge, and OSS activity
- mandatory e-invoicing / Peppol workflow matters for the answer
