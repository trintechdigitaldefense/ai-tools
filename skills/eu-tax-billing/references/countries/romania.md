# Romania — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, and small companies billing from Romania.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **19%**.
- Reduced rates exist in specific sectors; most professional services use 19%.
- Romania has a domestic VAT-registration threshold and separate cross-border triggers; verify the current threshold before relying on non-registration.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Romanian VAT** under reverse charge if the customer's country is the place of supply.
- Include VAT IDs where relevant.
- Typical wording: `Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-location VAT and may require **OSS**.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- invoice number and issue date
- seller legal name/address
- seller VAT ID if registered
- customer legal name/address
- customer VAT ID for reverse-charge B2B cases
- description, tax base, VAT treatment, total

Recommended practical fields:
- service period
- due date / payment terms
- contact details and bank details

Use this as a practical draft checklist only. If the seller is under a Romanian exemption or a RO e-Factura / local e-invoicing workflow may apply, verify the exact wording and data requirements.

## Quote notes
- Make clear whether values are before VAT or VAT included.
- Add validity period, scope, exclusions, and deposit terms.

## Sole trader / company note
- VAT-registration status matters more than entity form for invoice wording.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing is commonly **monthly or quarterly** depending on status.
- Keep support for intra-EU B2B treatment and any OSS sales.

## Escalate when
- the seller is near the threshold
- RO e-Factura or related reporting workflow matters operationally
- exact Romanian exemption wording is required
