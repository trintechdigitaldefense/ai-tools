# Italy — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, agencies, and small companies billing from Italy.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **22%**.
- Reduced rates exist in specific sectors; most professional services use 22%.
- Italy has special small-business / **regime forfettario** dynamics that materially affect invoice wording. Verify current eligibility and wording before relying on no-VAT treatment.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Italian VAT** under reverse charge if the customer's country is the place of supply.
- Include VAT IDs where relevant.
- Typical wording: `Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-location VAT and may require **OSS**.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- invoice number and issue date
- seller legal name/address
- seller VAT ID or tax identifier as relevant
- customer legal name/address
- customer VAT ID for intra-EU B2B reverse-charge cases
- description, tax base, VAT treatment, total

Recommended practical fields:
- service period
- payment terms
- contact details / bank details

Italy has strong local invoicing practice and e-invoicing workflows, especially for domestic operational setup. Treat the list above as practical guidance only. If using **regime forfettario** or other Italian exemption wording, verify the exact text and whether a platform-format workflow matters before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- Make clear whether amounts are before VAT or VAT included.
- Add validity period, scope, exclusions, and deposit / milestone terms.

## Sole trader / company note
- In Italy, the difference between ordinary VAT and **regime forfettario** can matter more than legal form for invoice wording.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing is commonly **quarterly or monthly** under ordinary VAT, with separate annual reporting obligations.
- Keep support for intra-EU B2B treatment and any OSS sales.

## Escalate when
- the seller uses **regime forfettario**
- e-invoicing format / routing matters operationally
- exact exemption wording or withholding interaction is needed
