# Finland — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, agencies, and small companies billing from Finland.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **25.5%**.
- Reduced rates exist in specific sectors; most professional services use the standard rate.
- Finland has a domestic VAT-registration threshold and reforms have been discussed; verify the current threshold before relying on non-registration.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Finnish VAT** under reverse charge if the customer's country is the place of supply.
- Include VAT IDs where relevant.
- Typical wording: `Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-location VAT and may require **OSS**.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- invoice number and issue date
- seller legal name/address
- seller VAT ID if registered
- customer legal name/address
- customer VAT ID for intra-EU B2B reverse-charge cases
- description, taxable amount, VAT treatment, total

Recommended practical fields:
- service period
- payment terms and due date
- contact details and bank details

Treat this as a practical split, not a final legal checklist. If the seller is not VAT-registered or uses a small-business relief, verify the current Finnish wording before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- Say whether the quote is before VAT or VAT included.
- Add validity period, scope, exclusions, and any advance-payment requirement.

## Sole trader / company note
- VAT treatment depends on registration status, not just business form.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing can be **monthly, quarterly, or annually** depending on turnover and status.
- Keep support for intra-EU B2B treatment and any OSS records.

## Escalate when
- the seller is near the threshold
- local no-VAT wording is needed
- filing cadence or reduced-rate treatment needs certainty
