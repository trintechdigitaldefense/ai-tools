# France — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, solo founders, and small service businesses billing from France.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **20%**.
- Common service-relevant reduced rates: **10%** and **5.5%** only in specific sectors; most professional services stay at 20%.
- Domestic VAT threshold: France's **franchise en base** thresholds have changed and been debated recently. Older guidance often cites **€34,400 for services** and **€85,800 for goods**; verify the current threshold before relying on it.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without French VAT** under reverse charge if the customer is a taxable business and the place of supply is the customer's country.
- Put both VAT numbers on the invoice where relevant.
- Typical wording: `Autoliquidation / Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** are generally taxed where the consumer is located. The EU-wide **€10,000** cross-border threshold and **OSS** may matter quickly.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- sequential invoice number
- issue date
- seller legal name/address
- seller VAT number if VAT-registered
- customer legal name/address
- customer VAT number for reverse-charge B2B cases
- service date or period if different from issue date
- net amount, VAT rate/treatment, total due
- payment terms and late-payment terms if used
- if VAT not charged because of the small-business regime, add the appropriate **franchise en base** wording rather than a VAT rate

Recommended practical fields:
- email / phone
- bank details
- PO/reference number

Treat the list above as a practical split between likely mandatory/core details and commonly useful extras. If a French exemption wording or extra identifier is uncertain, verify before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- Quotes are commonly called **devis**.
- State validity period, scope, exclusions, payment schedule/deposit, and whether amounts are HT or TTC.
- If the quote becomes binding on acceptance, make that explicit.

## Sole trader / company note
- French micro / small-business VAT handling can differ sharply depending on whether the business is under **franchise en base** or fully VAT-registered. Confirm the regime before drafting invoice wording.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- Expect VAT filing to be **monthly, quarterly, or under a simplified regime** depending on turnover/regime.
- Keep invoice numbering tight and preserve supporting evidence for cross-border B2B and OSS positions.

## Escalate when
- the seller is near or over the current **franchise en base** threshold
- domestic vs export vs OSS treatment is mixed in the same period
- the invoice needs a French exemption wording you are not certain about
- e-invoicing / e-reporting rollout timing matters operationally
