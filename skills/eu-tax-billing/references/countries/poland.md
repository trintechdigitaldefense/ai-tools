# Poland — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, agencies, and small companies billing from Poland.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **23%**.
- Reduced rates exist in specific sectors; most professional services use 23%.
- Poland has a domestic VAT-registration threshold and separate cross-border / digital-service triggers; verify the current threshold before relying on non-registration.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Polish VAT** under reverse charge if the customer's country is the place of supply.
- Include VAT IDs where relevant.
- Typical wording: `Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-location VAT and may require **OSS**.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- invoice number and issue date
- seller legal name/address
- seller VAT ID if registered
- customer legal name/address
- customer VAT ID for intra-EU B2B reverse-charge cases
- description, taxable amount, VAT treatment, total

Recommended practical fields:
- service period
- payment terms
- contact details / bank details

Treat the list above as a practical split between core details and useful extras. If the seller is outside VAT due to an exemption, verify the exact Polish wording and whether a local business number or platform-specific data point should also appear.

## Quote notes
- State whether prices are net or VAT included.
- Add validity period, scope, assumptions, and any advance-payment terms.

## Sole trader / company note
- VAT status and local invoicing workflow matter more than entity type for document wording.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing is commonly **monthly or quarterly** depending on status.
- Keep support for intra-EU B2B treatment and any OSS sales.

## Escalate when
- the seller is near the threshold
- exact exemption wording or extra local invoice fields are needed
- KSeF or other national platform workflow affects operations
