# Hungary — billing / quotes / VAT

## Overview
For freelancers, consultants, contractors, and small companies billing from Hungary.

## VAT basics
- Standard VAT: **27%**.
- Reduced rates exist in specific sectors; most professional services use 27%.
- Hungary has domestic small-business and VAT-registration frameworks that can change; verify the current threshold and regime wording before relying on no-VAT treatment.

## Cross-border rule of thumb
- **B2B services to another EU business**: usually invoice **without Hungarian VAT** under reverse charge if the customer's country is the place of supply.
- Include VAT IDs where relevant.
- Typical wording: `Reverse charge — Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC`.

## B2C / digital note
- **B2C digital services** generally follow customer-location VAT and can make **OSS** relevant quickly.

## Invoice fields / wording notes
Usually core / likely mandatory in practice:
- invoice number and issue date
- seller legal name/address
- seller VAT ID if VAT-registered
- customer legal name/address
- customer VAT ID for intra-EU B2B reverse-charge cases
- description, taxable amount, VAT treatment, total

Recommended practical fields:
- service period
- due date / payment terms
- contact details and bank details

Hungary can have operationally important invoice-reporting and software-workflow requirements. Treat this as a practical checklist only, and verify exact wording, reporting scope, and system requirements before finalizing.

## Quote notes
- State whether prices are before VAT or VAT included.
- Add validity period, scope, exclusions, and any deposit terms.

## Sole trader / company note
- Confirm VAT status and invoicing setup directly; do not infer from entity type.

## Filing cadence / admin rhythm
- VAT filing is often **monthly or quarterly** depending on status.
- Keep support for intra-EU supplies and any OSS sales.

## Escalate when
- the seller relies on a Hungarian small-business regime
- online invoice reporting or local software rules matter operationally
- exact exemption wording is uncertain
